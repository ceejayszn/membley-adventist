# Membley Adventist Church — Customization Rules

These guidelines apply to all future coding and design tasks for the Membley Adventist Church platform.

## Design & Color Rules
- Use only the established theme palette:
  - `--primary`: `#002f5d`
  - `--primary-dark`: `#001a35`
  - `--primary-light`: `#0d4b85`
  - `--accent`: `#d99e1a` (Church Gold)
- Avoid glowing animations, shimmering keyframes, neon greens, and rainbow gradients. Keep designs solid, clean, and elegant.
- Always guarantee high text contrast: pure white (`#ffffff`) on dark backgrounds and deep navy/slate on light backgrounds.

## Poster & Media Rules
- Never crop event posters. Display full uncropped images with `max-width: 440px; height: auto; object-fit: contain;`.
- Make posters clickable to navigate to detailed event or RSVP pages.

## Button Standards
- Fill Info & RSVP: Theme Blue (`#0d4b85`), white text.
- WhatsApp Share: Clean Green (`#16a34a`), white text.

## RSVP Form Rules
- Full name and phone are mandatory.
- If attendees > 1, generate mandatory name fields for each attendee.
- Manual text typing for church of origin.
- Confirmation shows "✨ Feel at the feet of Jesus ✨" and Psalm 95:1–2.

## Layout & Footer Rules
- Footer must be tiny and compact, taking at most half a page on mobile and desktop.
